Predict house prices using machine learning

Note: This model uses the California Housing dataset as a baseline. Predictions are adjusted for all 50 US states based on average home prices. Prices are predicted in hundreds of thousands of dollars (e.g., 2.5 = $250,000).
Field Descriptions:
  • State: Select the US state where the property is located. Predictions are adjusted based on state average home prices.
  • Median Income: Median household income in the block group (in tens of thousands of dollars). Example: 8.3 = $83,000/year
  • House Age: Median age of houses in the block group (in years). Older houses may be less expensive.
  • Average Rooms: Average number of rooms per household in the block group. Includes all rooms (bedrooms, living rooms, etc.). Limited to one decimal place.
  • Average Bedrooms: Average number of bedrooms per household (whole numbers only).
  • City Name: Name of the city where the property is located.
  • Street Name: Name of the street where the property is located.
  • Average Occupancy: Average number of people per household (whole numbers only). Higher occupancy may indicate more affordable areas.
